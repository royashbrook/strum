Strum listens for the six open strings in standard guitar tuning. Flat means tighten gently; sharp means loosen gently.

Prototype, not a finished tuner. Noise and voices can give false readings. A question mark means the sound is uncertain. Low E overtones overlap B and high E: try those strings separately when shown as uncertain.

Audio is processed on this device, never recorded or uploaded. Listening stops when you leave the tuner or switch apps.
